# TALRIA LIMITED DMCC — Corporate & Clinical Web Platform

Official website for **TALRIA LIMITED DMCC**, the global medical technology, intellectual property holding, and licensing enterprise headquartered in the Dubai Multi Commodities Centre (Dubai, UAE; transferred from Isle of Man).

The platform showcases the ground-breaking airway innovations developed by **Dr. Muhammed Aslam Nasir** (MBBS, FRCA, Consultant Anaesthetist & Macewen Medalist), including the human **i-gel®** supraglottic airway device and the veterinary species-specific **v-gel®** airway portfolio.

---

## Key Features

1. **Enterprise & Technology Showcase:**
   - Detailed coverage of **i-gel®** (licensed globally to Intersurgical Ltd) used in routine anaesthesia and emergency CPR across 100+ countries.
   - Detailed coverage of **v-gel®** (developed with Docsinnovent Ltd) for cats, rabbits, dogs, and horses, preventing tracheal tears and anaesthesia mortality.
   - Industrial and clinical need analysis comparing non-inflatable anatomical gel-cuffs against traumatic inflatable balloons.

2. **Executive Leadership & Board of Directors:**
   - **Dr. Muhammed Aslam Nasir**: Managing Director & Chief Inventor (Macewen Medalist, inventor of i-gel® & v-gel®, founder of Docsinnovent & Light4Life).
   - **Talha Nasir**: Chief Operating Officer (COO) (BSc Marine Biology and Environmental Sciences).
   - **Mr. Tuaha Nasir**: Director of Business Development (Bsc in business management & economics).
   - **Mr. Adam Nasir**: Financial Director (fiscal governance, IP valuation, capital allocation, DMCC corporate compliance).
   - **Mr. Danyal Nasir**: Director of Business Development (hospital and veterinary clinic network integration, academic partnerships).

3. **Interactive Airway Selection & Sizing Calculator:**
   - Real-time tool allowing clinicians and veterinary doctors to select patient domain (Human, Cat, Rabbit, Dog), configure patient weight, and receive instant recommendations for device size, color code, suction catheter gauge (Fr), optical tube conduit compatibility, and expected seal pressures.

4. **Clinical Evidence & Intellectual Property:**
   - Comprehensive summaries of landmark multi-center trials (AIRWAYS-2 in JAMA, Difficult Airway Society guidelines, Resuscitation studies).
   - Global patent registry (USPTO, EPO, WIPO) for the Nasir Laryngeal Airway Device and veterinary 3D anatomical bowls.

5. **Netlify Serverless Form Integration:**
   - Corporate and clinical partnership inquiry form supporting OEM licensing, hospital group tenders, veterinary distributor requests, and clinical trials.

---

## Tech Stack

- **Framework:** TanStack Start (full-stack React SSR + TanStack Router)
- **Frontend:** React 19, TypeScript
- **Styling:** Tailwind CSS 4 (`@tailwindcss/vite`)
- **Icons:** Lucide React
- **Forms:** Netlify Forms (with static SSR skeleton detection)
- **Deployment:** Netlify

---

## Local Development

### Prerequisites
- Node.js 22+
- pnpm (recommended) or npm

### Installation
```bash
pnpm install
```

### Running Locally
```bash
pnpm run dev
```
Open [http://localhost:3000](http://localhost:3000) in your browser.
